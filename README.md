Tagline

Code in Your Language. Learn Anywhere. Build Your Future.

Overview

KodeMamas is an offline-first, multilingual coding education platform designed for South African township, rural, and underserved communities. Built to work on low-end Android devices and unreliable internet connections, KodeMamas empowers girls, mothers, students, and first-time tech learners with accessible coding education, mentorship, and career support.

The platform combines coding lessons, AI-powered learning assistance, mentorship, and job-readiness tools into one lightweight mobile experience that works both online and offline.

---

Mission

To democratize access to coding education across South Africa by providing affordable, culturally relevant, multilingual, and offline-first technology that empowers communities to participate in the digital economy.

---

Vision

To become Africa's leading multilingual coding education ecosystem, creating pathways from learning to employment while increasing digital inclusion and representation of women in technology.
